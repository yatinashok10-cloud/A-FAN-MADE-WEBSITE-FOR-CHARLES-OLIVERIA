Charles
Oliveira

Brazilian jiu-jitsu black belt from Guarujá. Former UFC lightweight champion, current BMF champion, and the man with the most finishes and most submission wins in UFC history.

See the next fight: UFC 335, Dec 12

From the streets of Guarujá to the belt

Charles grew up in a modest home in Guarujá, Brazil, and found jiu-jitsu as a teenager. The mat became his school and his way out.

He reached the UFC in 2010, still in his early twenties. The road was rough. He was doubted, written off and knocked down more than most fighters ever are, and each time he rebuilt and kept winning.

In May 2021 he stopped Michael Chandler to win the lightweight title, then beat Dustin Poirier in December to defend it. After losing the belt he kept coming back. In March 2026, at 36, he out-grappled Max Holloway over five rounds to take the BMF title.

His record stands at 37-11 with one no contest, and 22 of those wins came by submission.
