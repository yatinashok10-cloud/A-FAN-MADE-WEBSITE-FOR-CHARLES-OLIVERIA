Experience

UFC FIGHTER

2010 - present

Charles Oliveira’s story begins far from the bright lights of Las Vegas, on the streets of Guarujá, Brazil, where he grew up in a modest home and found jiu-jitsu as a teenager. The mat became his escape, his school, and his future. He reached the UFC in 2010 while still in his early twenties, but the road was never smooth. He was doubted, written off, and knocked down more times than most fighters ever have to face, and every time, people said his time had passed. Instead of quitting, he rebuilt himself, moved up to lightweight, and kept winning. In 2021 he stopped Michael Chandler to become UFC Lightweight Champion, then defended the belt against Dustin Poirier that December. Even after losing the title and falling short in big fights, he kept coming back. In March 2026, at 36, he out-grappled Max Holloway over five rounds to win the BMF title, proof that his story is far from over. With a 37-11 record that includes 22 submission wins, UFC records for most finishes and most submission victories, and a fresh eight-fight contract, he is now set to defend the belt against Diego Lopes at UFC 335. Oliveira reminds us that falling down is not failure, giving up is, and that a kid from Guarujá can fight his way into history.
